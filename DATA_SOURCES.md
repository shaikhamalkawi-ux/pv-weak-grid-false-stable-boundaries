# External measurement sources (not redistributed)

The following public datasets have **separate** scientific roles in the associated study. We do not mirror their raw content here; consult the official source and any reuse terms.

| Data source | DOI | Restricted role |
|---|---|---|
| NREL/OEDI PVDAQ | [10.25984/1846021](https://doi.org/10.25984/1846021) | Scalar measured irradiance operating-level audit |
| Oahu Solar Measurement Grid | [10.7799/1052451](https://doi.org/10.7799/1052451) | Multi-site measured irradiance geometry input |
| OEDI IBR transient data library | [10.25984/2228281](https://doi.org/10.25984/2228281) | Measured dq-admittance interaction-root diagnostics |
| PV Inverter Experimental Dataset Version 2 | [10.7799/2205350](https://doi.org/10.7799/2205350) | Independent 20-kW inverter operating-envelope audit |

These measurements come from different systems and do not validate the 40×10 benchmark's critical short-circuit ratio or a universal 2.5% guard.
